About us

Becks & Decker is an industrial automation solutions provider focused on improving efficiency, reliability, and traceability in modern production environments. Our systems combine robotics, intelligent control, and real-time monitoring to support high-performance manufacturing lines.

We design operator-friendly interfaces and role-based dashboards that help production teams, engineers, and managers make faster and better decisions based on accurate process data.

Support

Our technical support team assists operators and engineers with system usage, troubleshooting, and configuration of production equipment and software tools. We also support continuous improvement and system optimization.

For critical production incidents, response times are prioritized to minimize downtime and ensure safe and continuous operation.

News

Stay up to date with the latest platform improvements, system upgrades, and feature releases. New dashboards, performance indicators, and monitoring tools are continuously added to enhance production visibility.

Recent updates include improved role-based access control, enhanced process analytics, and expanded maintenance diagnostics.

Help

The help section provides guidance on using dashboards, interpreting production metrics, and navigating the system interface.

For additional assistance, users can consult the built-in documentation or contact the helpdesk for personalized support.
